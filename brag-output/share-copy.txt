LinkedIn
--------
If you sell 1:1 time (mentoring, coaching, portfolio reviews), booking usually means a DM thread, a calendar link and a payment request sent separately.

BookMyMeet puts all of that on one page:
- Your own URL, with your sessions and your prices
- Slots worked out from your working hours, your Google Calendar, your leave and your existing bookings, so nothing gets double-booked
- Payment through your own Razorpay account, with no platform cut
- A Google Meet invite created when the booking is confirmed

Setup is four steps: profile, working hours, one session, gateway.

https://1v1-meeting-smart-meeting-booking.vercel.app
